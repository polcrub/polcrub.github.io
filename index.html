About

Barcelona, Spain

Catalan, Spanish (native) · English (fluent)

My interest in engineering started with a Meccano set when I was five. Each Christmas, I wanted more pieces and replacement motors for the ones I had burnt out. I wasn’t very good at following the manual, but I loved building my own little machines out of metal, wheels and wires.

Alongside that curiosity about machines and circuits, studying music gave me another way to explore and be creative. I’ve always enjoyed learning across different subjects, especially when I can put what I learn into practice.

I’m interested in modelling, control and practical engineering, as well as managing projects and bringing them to life. I want to work with people from different disciplines towards ambitious shared goals. I’m open to opportunities and relocation across Europe.

Experience

  1. Feb 2023 – Present · 3 years 8 months

    PhD Researcher · Optimization & Control

    Institut de Robòtica i Informàtica Industrial (IRI), CSIC–UPC · Barcelona

    I develop models and predictive control strategies for hydrogen refuelling stations, including a readiness model for cascaded storage. My research is based on the Zaragoza pilot under development and is funded by an FPU-UPC predoctoral fellowship.

  2. Aug 2021 – Jul 2025 · 3 years 11 months

    Research Support Engineer

    IRI (CSIC–UPC) · PTI-TRANSENER+

    I worked on plant simulation, rule-based operation and day-ahead scheduling of hydrogen production. I also integrated Simulink and Stateflow models into LabVIEW on CompactRIO for software-in-the-loop testing.

  3. Jun 2019 – May 2021 · 1 year 11 months

    Junior Product Manager · Renewables

    Groupe Atlantic · Spain & Portugal

    I supported the aerothermal, solar photovoltaic and solar thermal product ranges in Spain and Portugal. My work included market studies, technical documentation, training, supplier coordination and product data management. I also helped develop a kit combining photovoltaics and an aerothermal heat pump.

  4. UPC MotoStudent Electric

    E3-Team · EPSEVG–UPC · Vilanova i la Geltrú

    I spent five years with E3-Team, UPC Vilanova’s student team for the MotoStudent Electric competition.

    1. Team Lead

      Jul 2018 – Sep 2021 · 3 years 2 months

      I led the team as we rebuilt it from the ground up, coordinating vehicle integration and track testing and making internal knowledge transfer a priority. We doubled membership and the budget for that edition through new sponsorships and increased university support.

    2. Electronics Technician

      Oct 2016 – Jul 2018 · 1 year 9 months

      I worked on battery sizing and electric-drive modelling, and built and tested electric powertrain subsystems.
